Our 10-Point Hygiene Charter

We treat your wellness and safety as sacred. Here is our stringent hospital-grade sanitation protocol.

🛡️

Autoclave Tool Sterilization

Every scissor, cuticle pusher, and clipper head is sealed and heat-sterilized at 134°C between clients.

✨

Single-Use Disposables

Fresh disposable towels, salon capes, facial sponges, and waxing spatulas are provided for each guest.

💨

HEPA Air Purification

Continuous medical-grade air scrubbers capture chemical fumes, hair particles, and airborne impurities.

🧪

Patch Test Protocol

Mandatory 24-hour allergy patch testing for sensitive scalp clients before all chemical and color services.

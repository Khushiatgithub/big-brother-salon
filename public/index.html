Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know prior to booking your luxury salon visit.

While we happily accommodate walk-in guests based on chair availability, we strongly encourage reserving your preferred time slot online or via WhatsApp, especially for chemical hair services, bridal packages, and weekends.
Our Brazilian Keratin Smoothening lasts between 4 to 6 months depending on your hair texture and aftercare routine. We recommend using sulfate-free Kérastase or L'Oréal shampoo and conditioner for maximum longevity.
For wedding dates during peak wedding season (October to March), we recommend booking at least 2 to 4 months in advance. We provide complimentary pre-bridal skin consultations and hair trial sessions in our VIP Bridal Suite.
We are centrally located on Main Bazaar Road, Paharganj, just 5 minutes from New Delhi Railway Station and 3 minutes from RK Ashram Marg Metro Station. Valet assistance and nearby dedicated parking spaces are provided for our guests.
We follow strict single-use disposable cartridge and spatula protocols (zero double-dipping). All facial bowls and stainless steel instruments undergo medical autoclave sterilization before every single client session.
